### [US and Nine Countries Sign San Francisco Declaration on AI Supply Chains](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesman/2026/11/the-san-francisco-declaration-on-the-secure-foundations-of-enduring-prosperity/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The United States and Australia, India, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates, and the United Kingdom signed the San Francisco Declaration on November 15, 2026, committing to coordinate policy to protect sensitive technologies and critical infrastructure supporting superintelligence \(SI\) supply chains. The declaration establishes five working groups covering innovation policy frameworks, SI applications in key sectors, full-stack supply-chain collaboration, safe havens and supply-chain security hubs, and SI security and resilience. The State Department release presents this as a policy-coordination framework rather than a shipped capability or binding rule, and it does not disclose funding, compliance mechanisms, or enforcement measures.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 16, 10:40

> **「Background」** The declaration reflects a broader U.S. effort to treat advanced AI, compute infrastructure, and critical supply chains as matters of economic and national security. It extends earlier policy coordination on semiconductors and export controls into a multilateral framework for superintelligence-related technology.
