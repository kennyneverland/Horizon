### [China proposes whole-vehicle battery recycling for EVs](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3786200) ⭐️ 7.0/10

China’s commerce ministry and eight other departments proposed on Nov. 17 to revise scrapped-vehicle recycling rules so new energy vehicles and their power batteries are retired together, and to study a digital ID system for batteries. The plan builds on April interim rules requiring a scrapped EV to be treated as missing if its battery is absent; detailed management measures have not been finalized.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 18, 12:22

> **「Background」** China’s existing vehicle-scrapping rules already require recyclers to record power-battery codes and flows, while the April interim measures treat a scrapped new-energy vehicle without its battery as a missing vehicle; the nine-department proposal would build on that by requiring whole-vehicle-and-battery scrapping and creating a unique digital ID for each battery pack. The detailed management methods, including rules for battery-swap models, are still being developed.

> **「Impact」** If adopted, it would require NEV makers, battery producers, repair and swap operators, and recyclers to report battery movements, raising compliance costs but helping formal recycling channels track retired batteries and reduce illegal resale.
