# Financial news profile

The reader is a financial-market investor. They follow the major equity markets in this order of weight: **mainland China first, then the United States, Japan, Korea and Taiwan.** They are sensitive to AI hardware, AI applications, the upstream and downstream chains behind both, and to whatever technology theme is currently repricing a listed name.

When an item is an industry story rather than a macro one, it belongs here only if the industry is one this digest tracks:

- **AI hardware and compute** — accelerators and chips, servers and racks, data-centre capacity and construction, power generation and grid equipment, cooling, optical and copper interconnect, advanced packaging, memory, semiconductor equipment and materials.
- **AI applications** — model and agent products, enterprise AI deployment, pricing and unit economics, vertical AI in healthcare, finance, education, media and industry, consumer AI services.
- **Other technology industries** — consumer electronics; brain-computer interfaces; automotive technology; automotive manufacturing; financial technology; cybersecurity; telecommunications; AI in healthcare; robotics and embodied AI.

Otherwise the item must state how it transmits into a major index, a broad listed peer group, or one of those industries.

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding money, markets, companies, or economic policy **and** the item can plausibly move the price of a major equity market, a listed company, or a technology supply chain.

Typical items include macroeconomic data, interest rates, inflation, employment, fiscal policy, stocks, bonds, currencies, commodities, company earnings, financing, mergers and acquisitions, valuations, financial regulation, and industry policy with clear economic consequences.

For technology companies, route earnings, financing, valuation, acquisitions, and material business changes here. Route product releases, research results, software updates, security incidents, and engineering developments to the technology news profile.

Do not route these here:

- **Single-brand or non-listed business operations with no read-through.** A restaurant chain's sales pressure, a private company's employee benefits, a retailer's store count, a workplace or HR story — unless the item states an effect on a major index, a listed peer, or a technology chain, it has no price for this reader to act on.
- **Consumer, lifestyle and labour stories** that do not change a company's earnings outlook or a sector's cost structure in a way an investor can price.
- **Industry stories outside the tracked industries.** Sales, pricing or consolidation in restaurant, retail, steel, cement, property, agriculture, utilities or general manufacturing, unless the item states a transmission into a major index, a broad listed peer group, or one of the industries listed above.
- Routine price movements, unsupported market rumours, promotional investment content, and generic personal-finance advice.
