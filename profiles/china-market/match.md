# China markets profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding mainland China or Hong Kong equities, the companies listed there, or the policy and capital flows that move them.

Typical items include A-share and Hong Kong market moves with a stated driver, company earnings and guidance for Chinese issuers, regulatory actions against a sector, IPO and refinancing activity, Stock Connect flows, foreign institution rating changes on Chinese names, and industry policy with a direct read-through to listed companies.

For a global macro or non-China market story, use the general financial news profile. For product releases, research results, and engineering developments at Chinese technology companies, use the technology news profile unless the item's main value is its effect on listed equities.
