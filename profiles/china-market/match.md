# China markets profile

The reader is a financial-market investor whose **first market is mainland China**, with Hong Kong alongside it, and who also holds US, Japan, Korea and Taiwan exposure. They are sensitive to AI hardware, AI applications, the chains behind both, and to whatever technology theme is currently repricing a listed name.

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding mainland China or Hong Kong equities, the companies listed there, or the policy and capital flows that move them.

Typical items include A-share and Hong Kong market moves with a stated driver, company earnings and guidance for Chinese issuers, regulatory actions against a sector, IPO and refinancing activity, Stock Connect flows, foreign institution rating changes on Chinese names, and industry policy with a direct read-through to listed companies.

A non-China event belongs here when the transmission to Chinese listings is concrete — same product, same customers, substitutable capacity, a named Chinese beneficiary, or a stated pricing-power shift. Without that link it goes to the general financial news profile.

For a global macro story with no stated China read-through, use the general financial news profile. For product releases, research results, and engineering developments at Chinese technology companies, use the technology news profile unless the item's main value is its effect on listed equities.

Do not route these here:

- **Single-brand or non-listed business operations** with no stated effect on a listed issuer, a sector's cost structure, or a tradable theme.
- **Consumer, lifestyle and labour stories** that no investor can price into an A-share or Hong Kong name.
- Market commentary with no driver, unexplained index moves, and stock-touting without a verifiable source.
