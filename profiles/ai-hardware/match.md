# AI hardware and compute profile

Use this profile for developments in the physical stack AI runs on: accelerators and chips, servers and racks, data-centre capacity and construction, power generation and grid equipment, cooling, optical and copper interconnect, advanced packaging, memory, semiconductor equipment and materials, and the capital spending that pays for them.

Typical items include capacity and capex announcements, supply agreements, pricing and lead-time moves, a new silicon or packaging generation, fab and data-centre buildouts, power or nuclear deals signed to feed compute, export controls that change supply, and vendor share shifts.

Do not use this profile for software-only releases, model benchmarks with no hardware consequence, general market commentary, or consumer-gadget reviews.
