# AI application profile

Use this profile for what AI is sold as and used for: model and agent products, enterprise deployment, pricing and unit economics, vertical applications in healthcare, finance, education, media and industry, consumer AI services, and the vendors competing for that demand.

Typical items include launches with paying customers, adoption and usage disclosures, subscription or token pricing changes, agent platforms, partnerships that put a model into a product, regulation that shapes deployment, and evidence that AI revenue is or is not materialising.

Do not use this profile for chip, data-centre, power or packaging supply-chain news, pure research papers, developer-tool changelogs, or finance-first market coverage.
